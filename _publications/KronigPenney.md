Disordered stealthy hyperuniform (SHU) systems are emerging as platforms for controlling classical and quantum transport because single-scattering contributions vanish in the stealthy regime. Here, to our knowledge, we present the first perturbation theory of one-dimensional quantum transport through a continuum of identical point scatterers with SHU positional disorder. Our perturbation theory indicates the exact cancellation of all lower-order terms due to the Fourier-space stealthy condition $S(q)=0$, and further suppression of the lowest nonvanishing contribution, at fourth order, to the Lyapunov exponent $\lambda(k)$ due to the real-space hyperuniform constraint $\lim_{D\to\infty}\sigma_D^2=c<\infty$. Our findings have universal implications for wave propagation in disordered SHU systems.
