We show that the recent works on Anderson localization when the number of spatial dimensions $d\to\infty$ can be framed into the scenario dubbed conformality lost, which occurs when two fixed points of the RG collide and become complex. This framing makes it natural for a two-parameter scaling flow à la Berezinskii--Kosterlitz--Thouless (BKT) to control all the observables of the theory as conjectured in [Vanoni et al., PNAS 121, e2401955121 (2024)]. We also use this as a starting point for an $\varepsilon$ expansion of the RG equations in $\varepsilon=1/d\ll 1$, and find phenomenological RG equations which fit the numerical results in dimensions $d\gtrsim 4$.
