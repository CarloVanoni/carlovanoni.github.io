Title: Hyperuniformity and the hidden order of disorder

Abstract: In this talk, I will give an introduction to the concept of hyperuniformity and discuss some of its consequences in disordered systems. I will first introduce hyperuniformity from a formal point of view, as the suppression of long-scale density fluctuations. I will then discuss how such systems can be numerically obtained and briefly mention some recent results related to materials science and some of its broader applications. I will then devote the last part of the talk to discussing how hyperuniformity arises in quantum lattice-walk spin chains.
